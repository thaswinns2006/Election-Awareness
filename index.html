About Elections

What elections are and why they form the backbone of a free society.

An election is a formal decision-making process by which a population chooses individuals to hold public office in a representative democracy.

In a democratic nation, elections ensure that power originates from the consent of the governed. Citizens hold leaders accountable and peacefully shape public policy.

Voter Guide

Eligibility & Required Documents

Ensure you meet legal criteria and bring valid identification on election day.

🎂

Age Requirement

Must be at least 18 years old on or before the qualifying date of the election year.

🪪

Citizenship Status

Must be a legally recognized citizen of the country in which the election is held.

📝

Electoral Roll Registration

Your name must be officially registered in the voter list of your polling constituency.

Accepted Photo ID Documents

Voters must present one of the following valid photo identity documents at the booth:
